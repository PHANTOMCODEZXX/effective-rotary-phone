    About

    Small business growth, without the guesswork

    Kaeti Enterprises started with a simple observation: small businesses are told they need to be on social media, but rarely shown what to post, how to measure it, or why it matters to their bottom line.

    So the studio was built around a different question — what does this channel need to do for the business? Every account begins with the numbers that matter to you: enquiries, saved posts, profile visits, booked calls, repeat customers.

    The approach is deliberately unglamorous. Publish consistently. Make the offer clear. Answer the questions your customers already have. Test the ads instead of guessing at them. Then look at what worked and do more of it.

    Consistency beats virality. Every time.

    Services

    Three ways to work together

    Take one service or combine all three. Either way, the work starts with the same question: what does social media need to do for your business?

    Social media management

    One team plans, publishes, and replies across your channels, so posting stops being the thing that slips.

    • Platform strategy for Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and LinkedIn
    • Scheduling and publishing on a monthly content calendar
    • Community management — comments and DMs answered in your voice
    • Monthly reporting on the numbers that matter to your business

    What results to expect A consistent presence you can rely on, and a clear monthly picture of what is working.

    Content creation

    Short-form video and copy built around what your customers actually ask before they buy.

    • Short-form video for Reels and TikTok, from concept to edit
    • Photography direction and shot lists for product and team content
    • Copywriting for captions, hooks, and profile pages
    • A content calendar you can plan the rest of your marketing around

    What results to expect More saves, shares, and profile visits, plus a library of content you can reuse.

    Paid advertising

    Meta and TikTok campaigns tested in small, measured steps before you commit more budget.

    • Meta and TikTok campaign setup, tracking, and pixel installation
    • Audience research and a structured creative testing plan
    • Hook, offer, and format variations tested against each other
    • Funnel optimization from first click to booked enquiry

    What results to expect A clearer cost per enquiry and a read on which creative earns its budget.

    Process

    How we work

    Four steps, run in the same order every time, so you always know what happens next.

    1. 01

      Discovery call

      A focused conversation about your business, your customers, and what growth needs to look like. You leave with a straight answer on whether this is the right fit.

    2. 02

      Strategy and content plan

      We map channels, offers, and posting rhythm to your goals, then build the first month of content before anything goes live.

    3. 03

      Creation and launch

      Shoot, edit, write, schedule. Campaigns go live with tracking in place, so results are measurable from day one.

    4. 04

      Optimize and report

      We review performance every month, do more of what worked, cut what did not, and agree next steps with you.
